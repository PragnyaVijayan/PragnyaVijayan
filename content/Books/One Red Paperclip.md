### One Red Paperclip: Or How an Ordinary Man Achieved His Dream with the Help of a Simple Office Supply
**By Kyle Macdonald**

This is the next book on my reading list!

**Description:**
> Kyle MacDonald had a paperclip. One red paperclip, a dream, and a lot of ingenuity. He used the internet to trade his red paperclip for a fish-shaped pen, which he then traded for a doorknob, and so on... until he eventually traded his way up to a two-story farmhouse in Saskatchewan!

This book is a fantastic exploration of value, internet culture, and the power of human connection. I am excited to read it to learn more about achieving big goals through unconventional, iterative steps.

**My motivation**

I was inspired to read this book after watching seeing people repeat similar trades. In this day and age, financial independence and security is fragile. About 67% of Gen Z's are struggling to cover their housing costs and what was once an acheivable American Dream is now a pipedream for many. While my biggest motivation, if I were to repeat this experiment, would be to trade up to an house, I also think this book can teach me a lot about the lost art of bartering. 

Also, I recently moved to the Greater Boston area and I think this challenge could be a fun way to meet new people and explore the area.
