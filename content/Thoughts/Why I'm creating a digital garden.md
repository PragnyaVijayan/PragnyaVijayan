---
title: Why I'm Creating a Digital Garden
---

I was inspired to revamp my personal portfolio into a [digital garden](https://carabros.medium.com/what-is-a-digital-garden-075628a2f312) for a few reasons.

### 1. Information Fatigue

First, I came across the idea of building a **personal knowledge management system**. As a software engineer and an avid hacker, I often experience cognitive overload while trying to process the information I encounter daily. 

The advent of coding assistants and LLMs has made writing easier, but the sheer volume of words I have to read has increased significantly. I often work with multiple agents simultaneously across various repositories, and I might have a terminal open just to learn about the latest exciting releases in the CS world. This constant exposure to information can lead to what I believe to be [cognitive overload and information fatigue](https://thecreativelife.net/second-brain/).

### 2. Outsourcing My Voice

Second, I’ve noticed a decline in my own writing. Writing is an underrated way to develop knowledge; it forces you to ingest information, process, distill, and convey it effectively. 

I used to write extensively in school, but that changed around early 2023, following the release of [ChatGPT](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/31/chatgpt-everything-released-from-the-ai-powered-chatbot-in-2022/). As LLMs became integrated into coursework, I found myself writing less and outsourcing more. These days, I don't write at all. I mainly use LLMs to write code, documentation, presentations, commit messages, and PR descriptions. 

> It seems that more and more, my voice and deep thinking disappear beyond using it at the beginning of a task to create a contextual, well-written-out prompt with predefined steps. But I don’t want to kiss the art of writing and making judgment goodbye.

### 3. Embracing the Unfinished

Lastly, I’ve always had a tendency to wait until something is fully polished before sharing it. But given how quickly ideas evolve, that approach no longer feels right. **An unfinished thought can still be valuable.** 

Instead of waiting, I want to share ideas as they develop and connect them over time, reflecting the non-linear way we actually learn.

---

### The Solution

My solution to these problems is to create a personal knowledge management system so that I can effectively preserve the information I encounter in a **second brain**. 

I intend to write most of the words on this website to both improve my writing skills and ensure that I fully comprehend the material I’m writing. But I will use AI to help me polish my writing, organize, and make connections between ideas. I’m intentionally making the page an aggregation of *unpolished thoughts* and *open-ended notes* that I can quickly jot down. I hope that through this process, I can grow my knowledge and understanding.
